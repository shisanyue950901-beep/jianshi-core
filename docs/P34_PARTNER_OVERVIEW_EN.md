# JianShi Partner Overview

## What is JianShi?

JianShi (见势) is a Chinese-language opportunity discovery and decision platform for individual entrepreneurs and business content creators.

We help users discover promising business opportunities, compare them, understand the evidence behind them, and decide which opportunities are worth deeper research.

## What JianShi is not

JianShi is not an acquisition marketplace and does not broker transactions.

We do not want to copy or mirror full marketplace listings, private seller information, confidential financial documents, customer data, or NDA-protected information.

## How we would like to work with marketplaces

Our preferred model is:

1. Reference a limited set of approved public listing fields.
2. Turn those fields into high-level discovery, comparison and aggregate trend analysis.
3. Clearly attribute the source marketplace.
4. Link interested users directly back to the original listing/platform.
5. Follow any field restrictions, attribution requirements, rate limits, caching rules and takedown requirements specified by the source.

## Why this may be useful to partners

JianShi focuses on helping Chinese-language users understand online business opportunities.

A partner marketplace keeps the full listing, seller relationship and transaction workflow, while JianShi acts as a discovery and education layer that can introduce new qualified traffic back to the source platform.

## Current stage

JianShi is currently in product validation.

We are reaching out early because we want to build within each source platform’s preferred data and attribution boundaries from the beginning rather than make assumptions later.
